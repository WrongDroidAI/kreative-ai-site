03 Your control

Present when you want it. Quiet when you do not.

You decide how active Your AI should be. Choose where it can contact you, what deserves a nudge, how often it checks in, and when it should stay quiet. Every outreach explains why it happened and gives you immediate control to receive more, less, or none of that kind.

Your information belongs to you. It is protected, never sold, never used to train shared AI models, and never shared without your permission.

In the appConversations, plans, progress, and a clear record of what Your AI remembers.
Email and notificationsUseful updates and check-ins without requiring you to keep the app open.
Text messagesShort, timely support when you explicitly enable it.
AI voice callsOptional conversations with clear AI identification, recognizable caller ID, and immediate opt-out.
